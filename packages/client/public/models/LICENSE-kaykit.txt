Models derived from KayKit asset packs by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com):
KayKit Adventurers Character Pack 2.0, KayKit Character Animations 1.1,
KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0 (the free pack and the paid EXTRA pack, both CC0 per their
License.txt) and KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0.

Credit is given anyway: "Kay Lousberg, www.kaylousberg.com".

The GLBs in this folder that come from these packs were assembled with
tools/models/kaykit_character.py (character mesh + selected animation clips,
cape recoloured as the "Team" material, scaled to 1 unit tall) and
tools/models/kaykit_prop.py (buildings normalised to one hex, props at tile scale, ground tiles
with a small palette nudge on the grass swatch) and tools/models/kaykit_compose.py (hills and
mountains composed onto a grass tile).
